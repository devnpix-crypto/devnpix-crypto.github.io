Slate

A modern dark theme for Utopia.

Use it
  1. In the game, click your username (top right) and choose Account Settings.
  2. Under Custom Theme, paste https://devnpix-crypto.github.io/ and click Apply.
Go back to the classic look

Tick Remove Custom Theme in Account Settings, or visit utopia-game.com/shared/reset_theme.
